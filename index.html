📖 What the Terms Mean

OD & OS

  • OD = right eye, OS = left eye — just old Latin abbreviations eye doctors still use

Sphere (the first number)

  • Tells you the main focusing power needed for that eye
  • Minus (−) number = nearsighted (myopia) — distant things look blurry
  • Plus (+) number = farsighted (hyperopia) — close-up things look blurry
  • The bigger the number (ignoring the sign), the stronger the correction needed

Cylinder & Axis (correcting astigmatism)

  • Astigmatism just means the front of the eye (cornea) is shaped a bit more like a football than a basketball, so light bends unevenly and things can look slightly blurry or stretched at any distance
  • Cylinder = how much correction is needed to fix that uneven shape (always paired with an axis)
  • Axis = the angle (measured in degrees, 0–180°) that tells the lens maker which direction to place that correction
  • Astigmatism is extremely common and glasses fix it easily — it is not a disease or an eye health problem
✅ What This Means for Areen

  • Overall, this is a mild, very normal prescription — astigmatism is what's mainly being corrected here, not serious nearsightedness or farsightedness
  • This is not a "bad" result — it's an extremely common finding, especially in teens, and is fully correctable with the glasses already prescribed
